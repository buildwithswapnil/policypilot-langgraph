# PolicyPilot Employee Handbook

## Casual Leave Policy

Every full-time employee receives 12 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave must be requested through the HR portal. More than three consecutive casual leave days require manager approval.

## Sick Leave Policy

Every full-time employee receives 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Employees taking more than two consecutive sick leave days must submit a medical certificate. Employees on probation can use accrued sick leave.

## Work From Home Policy

Managers can approve the WFH requests. Employees may work from home for up to two days per week with manager approval. Work-from-home requests must be submitted at least one working day in advance. Employees must remain available during normal working hours.

## Payroll Policy

Monthly salary is credited on the final working day of each month. Payroll changes must be submitted before the twentieth day of the month. Changes submitted after the cut-off are processed in the following payroll cycle.

## Reimbursement Policy

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction. An invoice or receipt is mandatory. Reimbursement requests above ₹10,000 require department-head approval.
